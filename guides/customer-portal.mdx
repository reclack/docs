The portal is a page your customers sign into with the wallet they paid from.
They see their plans, invoices, receipts and payments, and can cancel where you
allow it. There is no password to set and nothing for your support team to
reset.

## Switching it on

Enable it in the dashboard under **Settings**, then **Portal**, or through the
API:

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://api.reclack.com/v1/merchants/mer_2Pb8/portal" \
  -H "Api-Key: $RECLACK_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "enabled": true,
    "allow_cancellation": true,
    "headline": "Your billing with Acme Labs"
  }'
```

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled` | Whether the portal answers at all. |
| `allow_cancellation` | Whether customers may end a plan themselves. |
| `headline` | The line they see at the top of the page. |

## How a customer signs in

They prove the wallet. The page asks them to sign a short challenge, and the
session that comes back is scoped to that one customer's records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="They open your portal URL">
    `https://pay.reclack.com/acme`, where `acme` is your workspace slug.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They connect a wallet and sign a challenge">
    No password, no email link, no account to create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The session is scoped on the server">
    Every query is filtered by that scope on our side, not by what the page
    happens to ask for.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What they can do

| They can | They cannot |
| --- | --- |
| See every plan with your business | See anything belonging to another customer |
| Download invoice PDFs | Change an invoice |
| Open a receipt for any payment | Move money anywhere other than to you |
| Follow a payment to the block explorer | |
| Cancel a plan, when you allow it | |

Cancellation is sent from their own wallet. Reclack confirms the chain agreed
before marking the subscription cancelled, and your application hears
`subscription.cancelled`.

## Branding

The portal carries the same logo, tint and button colour as your checkout,
receipts and emails. Set it once under **Settings**, then **Branding**.

<Note>
Reclack never asks a customer for a seed phrase or a private key, in the portal
or anywhere else. If a page claiming to be yours does, it is not ours.
</Note>

## What it saves you

The portal exists to empty a specific part of your inbox:

- Requests for a copy of an invoice.
- Requests for a receipt.
- Questions about when a plan renews.
- Cancellations that would otherwise be a support ticket.
